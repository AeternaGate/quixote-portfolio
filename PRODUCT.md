## Users

Primary: freelance customers and employers who need high-quality solutions in software development — people with ideas and problems seeking someone to realize them.

## Product Purpose

A portfolio for Quixote.Dev that creates a strong first impression ("wow effect") and presents Quixote and their capabilities so convincingly that visitors with ideas or problems want to commission or hire them.

## Positioning

A portfolio that sells the developer's capabilities through demonstrated real projects (an AI-powered Telegram autoposting bot and an AI-tools website) rather than a generic résumé, so visitors perceive a proven partner who can turn an idea into a working solution.

## Operating Context

Visitors land, evaluate the developer's credibility and showcased projects, then reach out. Contact is the conversion goal.
